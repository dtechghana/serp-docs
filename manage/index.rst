Using sERP
##########

This section of the guide covers the core modules and features available in sERP. Each module corresponds to a section in the main navigation menu.

**Available Modules**

* :doc:`/manage/students` — Student bio data, registration, attendance, scholarships, health records, and disciplinary records
* :doc:`/manage/academic` — Classes, subjects, SBA, progress reports, assignments, curriculum, exam scheduling, and lesson plans
* :doc:`/manage/finance` — Fees, billing, payments, debtors, income & expenditure, and online payments
* :doc:`/manage/hr` — Staff records, payroll, PAYE, SSNIT, and regulatory compliance
* :doc:`/manage/sms` — Bulk SMS, bill reminders, payment notifications, and attendance alerts
* :doc:`/manage/canteen` — Canteen payment recording and store/inventory management
* :doc:`/manage/reports` — Attendance, finance, enrollment, and staff reports
* :doc:`/manage/teachers` — Teacher portal: assignments, notes, lesson plans, and attendance
* :doc:`/manage/communication` — In-app messaging, parent-teacher conferences, feedback, and discussion forums
* :doc:`/manage/security` — Multi-factor authentication and automated backups
